MISSION CONTROL

Every decision has an orbit.

A game where you design a spacecraft, pick an orbit and a rocket, launch it, and run the mission.

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Challenge: Space Mission Design Game

Play it: death150811.github.io/mission-control

1 / 7

The problem

  • Building a space mission means trading mass, power, orbit, rocket and money against each other.
  • Students almost never get to try it themselves.
  • So we made a game where you can.
2 / 7

What you do

  • Build a spacecraft in 3D and watch mass, power and cost change.
  • Choose the orbit and the rocket.
  • Launch, then run the mission day by day and handle problems.
  • At the end, see what went wrong and how to score higher.
3 / 7

Real physics under the hood

  • The rocket equation, orbits, radio links, eclipses, radiation and heat are calculated, not faked.
  • Miss the Mars launch window and you lose payload or time.
4 / 7

NASA data we used

  • NASA Planetary Fact Sheets for Earth, Moon and Mars.
  • NASA mission pages for real examples: LRO, MRO, MAVEN, OSIRIS-REx, Dawn, GRAIL, Landsat 9, Webb.
  • Every technology in the game points to a real one.
5 / 7

Why it matters

  • It teaches how real missions are planned, by playing.
  • 5 missions, 79 parts, 13 tests, 17 achievements.
  • Works in any browser. No install.
